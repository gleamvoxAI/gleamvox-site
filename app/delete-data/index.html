Delete my data

The keyboard keeps its data on your phone, and you delete it there. If you also created a Gleamvox account (only needed for Gleamvox AI), see the second part of this page.

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Gleamvox → Storage → Clear data. This removes learned words, settings and the clipboard history.
  2. Or uninstall the app: all its data is removed with it.

Words you added to Android's own user dictionary belong to the system: remove them in Android settings (Languages → Personal dictionary). Questions: support@gleamvox.com.

If you have a Gleamvox account

  • In the app open Settings → Gleamvox AI → Delete account, and confirm. An active subscription is cancelled first; then the account data is deleted.
  • Or write to support@gleamvox.com from the e-mail address of the account and ask us to delete it.
