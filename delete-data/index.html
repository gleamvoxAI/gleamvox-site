Delete my data

Gleamvox has no servers and no accounts, and it stores nothing about you outside your phone. Everything it keeps is on your device, and you delete it there:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Gleamvox → Storage → Clear data. This removes learned words, settings and the clipboard history.
  2. Or uninstall the app: all its data is removed with it.

Words you added to Android's own user dictionary belong to the system: remove them in Android settings (Languages → Personal dictionary). Questions: support@gleamvox.com.
