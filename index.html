Gleamvox

A private keyboard with smart suggestions

Gleamvox keeps what you type on your phone. It works offline and never sends your text anywhere.

Coming soon to Google Play

Private by design

No internet permission: nothing you type, say or copy can leave your phone. No ads, no analytics, no accounts.

Smart suggestions

Corrections and next-word suggestions from built-in dictionaries for many languages, all on your device. It learns your own words, but never in password fields.

Many languages

Switch languages with one key. Several layouts per language, emoji, symbols and a number row.

Clipboard and voice

Clipboard history with one-tap paste, pictures included. Voice input through your phone's own speech recognition.
