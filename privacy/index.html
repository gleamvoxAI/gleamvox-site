Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

No data leaves your device. Gleamvox has no internet permission and cannot send anything anywhere. It contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and it has no accounts.

What stays on your device. To suggest words, Gleamvox learns the words you type and keeps them, together with its dictionaries, your settings and the clipboard history (if you turn it on), only in the app's private storage on your phone. It does not learn in password fields, in fields that ask not to remember input, or in incognito mode. You can delete everything by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Contacts. If you turn on "Use contact names", Gleamvox reads the names of your contacts to suggest them while you type. The names are used only on your device and are never stored outside the system contacts or sent anywhere.

Voice input. When you tap the microphone, Gleamvox passes the sound to the speech recognition service of your phone (for example Google's). Gleamvox does not record, store or send the sound itself. How that service handles the sound is described in its provider's privacy policy.

Pictures from the clipboard. If you copy a picture, Gleamvox can show it in the suggestion strip and insert it into a field that accepts pictures. It is kept only in the app's private storage, as long as the clipboard history keeps it.

Children. Gleamvox is not directed at children.

Changes and contact. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: support@gleamvox.com.
