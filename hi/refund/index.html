Refund policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Free trial. The 7-day trial is free. If you cancel before it ends, you are not charged.

Refund within 14 days. If you are not satisfied, you can get a refund within 14 days after any payment, monthly or yearly. For the yearly plan this is a full refund of the payment within the same 14 days; after that the yearly payment is not refunded.

How to ask for a refund. Your order is placed by our reseller and merchant of record, Paddle. Request the refund through Paddle at paddle.net (the link is in your Paddle receipt e-mail). For questions and help, write to support@gleamvox.com.

Cancelling. You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Gleamvox AI until the end of the period you already paid for. After the 14 days, payments for periods that have already started are not refunded, except where the law of your country requires it.

How the money returns. A refund goes back to the card or payment method you used. It is processed by our payment provider and may take several business days to appear.

Contact. Questions and help about a payment or a refund: support@gleamvox.com.
