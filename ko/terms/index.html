Gleamvox AI. Gleamvox AI is an optional paid service (voice input with AI recognition and ✨ text editing) available to signed-in accounts with an active subscription or free trial, on up to 5 devices. The keyboard itself stays free under the GPL. AI results can be wrong; check them before you send them. Fair-use limits apply. We may refuse or stop the service where the law forbids it or the service is abused.

Account. You need a Gleamvox account for Gleamvox AI. Keep access to your e-mail address and Google account safe; you are responsible for what is done through your account.

Subscription, price and auto-renewal. Gleamvox AI costs $7.99 per month or $59.99 per year (see the pricing page; taxes may be added). The subscription renews automatically for the same period at the then current price until you cancel. You can cancel at any time; after that it is not renewed and works until the end of the paid period.

Free trial. A new account can start one free trial of 7 days. A payment card is required. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription starts and the first payment is taken. Only one trial per account.

Refunds. You can get a refund within 14 days after any payment; see our refund policy. Refunds are requested through Paddle.

Seller of record. Payments for Gleamvox AI are processed by our payment provider, Paddle, which acts as the merchant of record (the seller) and is responsible for the payment, taxes and invoices. Its terms apply to the payment.

Availability. Gleamvox AI is not sold in some countries, including Russia and Belarus.
