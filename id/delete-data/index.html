Hapus data saya

Gleamvox tidak punya server dan akun, dan tidak menyimpan apa pun tentang Anda di luar ponsel Anda. Semua yang disimpannya ada di perangkat Anda, dan Anda menghapusnya di sana:

  1. Buka Pengaturan Android → Aplikasi → Gleamvox → Penyimpanan → Hapus data. Ini menghapus kata yang dipelajari, pengaturan, dan riwayat papan klip.
  2. Atau copot aplikasi: semua datanya ikut terhapus.

Kata yang Anda tambahkan ke kamus pengguna milik Android adalah milik sistem: hapus di pengaturan Android (Bahasa → Kamus pribadi). Pertanyaan: support@gleamvox.com.

If you have a Gleamvox account

Until a translation is ready, this part is in English only. Where it differs from the text above (for example about accounts or network access), this English text applies.

  • In the app open Settings → Gleamvox AI → Delete account, and confirm. An active subscription is cancelled first; then the account data is deleted.
  • Or write to support@gleamvox.com from the e-mail address of the account and ask us to delete it. Payment records that our payment provider must keep by law stay with the provider.
