Gleamvox AI. Account. You need a Gleamvox account for Gleamvox AI. Keep access to your e-mail address and Google account safe; you are responsible for what is done through your account.

Subscription, price and auto-renewal. Gleamvox AI costs $7.99 per month or $59.99 per year (see the pricing page; taxes may be added). You can cancel at any time; after that it is not renewed and works until the end of the paid period.

Free trial. A new account can start one free trial of 7 days. A payment card is required. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription starts and the first payment is taken. Only one trial per account.
